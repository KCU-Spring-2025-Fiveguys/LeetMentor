LeetMentor Privacy Policy

Last updated: April 25, 2025

This Privacy Policy describes how LeetMentor ("we", "us", or "our") collects, uses, and shares your information when you use our Chrome extension.

Information We Collect

When you use the LeetMentor extension, we collect the following information:

  • Your code from LeetCode problems
  • The programming language you're using
  • The name of the LeetCode problem you're working on
  • Your feedback and interaction with the extension

How We Use Your Information

We use the information we collect to:

  • Provide code hints and improvement suggestions
  • Generate follow-up questions to help you learn
  • Create summaries of feedback for your reference
  • Improve our services and user experience

Data Storage

We store your feedback history and reminder preferences locally in your browser using Chrome's storage API. This data is not shared with our servers except when explicitly sending code for analysis.

Third-Party Services

LeetMentor uses a remote server at leetmentor.vercel.app for processing your code and generating hints. Your code is transmitted to this server when you request hints or improvements.

Data Retention

Feedback drafts are automatically deleted after 24 hours. Your feedback history is retained in your local browser storage until you clear it.

Your Rights

You can clear your stored data at any time by clearing your browser's storage or by uninstalling the extension.

Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update our Privacy Policy from time to time. We will notify you of any changes by posting the new Privacy Policy on this page.

Contact Us

If you have any questions about this Privacy Policy, please contact us at: support@leetmentor.com
